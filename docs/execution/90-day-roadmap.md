## 1. The Core Principle

You are **one person**. You have **~40-50 productive hours/week**. You have **90 days** before momentum from the planning phase decays.

Everything else — docs, strategies, analyses — is noise unless it drives one of these 4 outcomes:

| Workstream | 90-Day Goal |
|---|---|
| **A. Customer Discovery** | 20 interviews completed, 5 hot leads, real pricing data |
| **B. Hiring (CTO + Advisors)** | 2-3 advisors signed, CTO shortlist of 5+, 1-2 in deep diligence |
| **C. Operations (Name, Legal, Infra)** | Name picked, Delaware+India entity formed, bank + Stripe + Mercury set up |
| **D. Fundraising Prep** | 100-investor list, data room, 10 warm intros queued, deck v2 |

**Everything else is distraction.**

## 8. What NOT to Do (Anti-Patterns)

### ❌ Don't optimize prematurely
- Don't build the "perfect" tracker (any tracker > no tracker)
- Don't rebuild the pitch deck weekly (version it monthly)
- Don't rewrite the blueprint based on 2 interviews (wait for pattern)

### ❌ Don't delay the hard conversations
- Have the "co-founder vs CTO hire" conversation this week
- Have the "which legal entity" conversation this week
- Have the "fundraising vs bootstrap" conversation this week
- These block everything downstream

### ❌ Don't chase investor meetings before pre-reqs done
- Legal entity MUST be formed before SAFEs
- CTO (or clear path) MUST be visible to serious seed investors
- Discovery data MUST exist before pitch meetings
- Jumping gun = wasted first impression on key investors

### ❌ Don't do solo-founder things that destroy founders
- Don't work 70-hour weeks for 90 straight days (burnout = 2 months lost)
- Don't skip exercise + sleep
- Don't say YES to every advisor who offers help (quality > quantity)
- Don't open 15 workstreams simultaneously; stay in the 4

### ❌ Don't let perfection block shipping
- Pitch deck is NEVER done; v2 beats polished v10
- Prototype is NEVER done; discovery feedback > features
- Blueprint is NEVER done; strategy evolves

## 9. Burnout Prevention Protocol

Solo founders burn out around month 2-3. Build these guardrails NOW.

### Non-negotiable
- **7+ hours sleep nightly**
- **1 full day OFF per week** (hard stop, no exceptions)
- **30 min exercise 5× per week** (walk counts)
- **One meal a day eaten with another human** (not laptop)
- **One hour weekly** doing something unrelated to startup (hobby, music, reading fiction)

### Weekly ritual
- **Friday afternoon shutdown ritual** — close laptop by 5 PM
- **Saturday fully off** — don't check email until Sunday evening
- **Sunday evening 2-hr plan** for next week

### Red flags to watch for
- Sleep < 6 hours multiple nights
- Snapping at loved ones / friends
- Losing interest in the work
- Taking longer to make decisions
- Skipping food or exercise more than 2 days
- Not being able to describe what you did yesterday

**If any 3 of these fire, take a 3-day break immediately. Non-negotiable.**

## 10. External Accountability

Solo founders drift without accountability. Set these up:

### Weekly accountability call (30 min)
- Partner with one of your advisors or a founder friend
- Fixed day/time (e.g., Friday 3 PM)
- Share this week's wins + misses + next week's 3 priorities
- They push back when you're drifting

### Monthly founder dinner
- 5-6 founders at similar stage, same city
- Rotate hosting
- Share real numbers + real struggles

### Quarterly board of advisors call (post-advisor signing)
- 60 min, all 5 advisors on
- Pre-send update email 48 hrs ahead
- Make specific asks

## 13. Emergency Modes

### If customer discovery is dead (no responses by Week 3)
- Review outreach messages (likely too generic)
- Switch channels (cold email → warm intros only)
- Reduce target → smaller company list
- Try different persona (Factory GM easier than Brand VP)
- Hire a BDR/VA for cold outreach ($500-1k/mo on Upwork)

### If CTO search is dead by Week 6
- Lower bar on seniority (Staff Engineer → Senior Engineer)
- Add $20-50k signing bonus (comes from seed round)
- Increase equity grant (12% → 15%)
- Expand to international (remote-friendly)
- Partner with executive recruiter ($20-30k retainer, worth it for critical hire)

### If entity formation drags past Week 10
- Pay for expedited filing ($200-500 extra)
- Escalate with lawyer (threaten to switch firms)
- Form Delaware alone first; India subsidiary later (don't block on both)

### If you're overwhelmed by Week 4
- Cut one workstream temporarily (usually fundraising prep since no money needed yet)
- Delegate admin to a VA ($500-1k/mo)
- Have the "I'm burning out" call with a trusted advisor
- Take a 3-day break. Non-negotiable.

## 15. The Honest Truth About 90-Day Sprints

### What likely WILL happen
- You'll hit 80% of plan, not 100%
- One workstream will underperform (usually discovery or CTO)
- You'll have 1-2 bad weeks where you question everything
- An advisor you expected to sign will ghost you
- An investor you thought was warm will say no
- Your blueprint will need 1-2 significant revisions

### What probably WON'T happen
- Perfect execution on all 4 workstreams
- Finding the perfect CTO in week 4
- Zero rework on the pitch deck
- Smooth entity formation without a hiccup
- 100% of discovery interviews validating assumptions

### What is OK and even NECESSARY
- Changing your mind as data comes in
- Pivoting messaging after interview #10
- Firing a bad advisor relationship early
- Pushing back on a weak CTO candidate even in week 11

### What is NOT OK
- Hiding bad news from advisors / spouses
- Working 70+ hour weeks consistently
- Making the perfect enemy of the good
- Confusing activity with progress
