The problem

Apparel quality is still run on spreadsheets, WhatsApp, and paper.

Every party in the supply chain sees a different reality — and the gaps between them cost millions in rework, chargebacks, and lost trust. We built FABRIQ for the three people most affected.

For brands

You find out too late

By the time defects reach your DC, the goods are already on a ship. There's no good lever left — just a chargeback, a discount, or a markdown.

  • No visibility into inspection results until shipment
  • Rework and air-freight costs eat margin
  • Compliance evidence is scattered across emails
For factories

You can't prove quality

Every brand wants a different form in a different format. Your QC team juggles 15 spreadsheets a day — and still gets questioned when something slips.

  • Each buyer brings their own paper system
  • Line supervisors log defects on WhatsApp
  • Audit trail breaks the moment it leaves the floor
For 3P inspectors

You're stuck on paper

AQL samples get logged on carbon triplicates, re-entered into Excel at the hotel, uploaded to a client portal next morning. Three chances to lose data.

  • Paper forms · duplicate data entry · signature chase
  • No offline mode for factories without wifi
  • Reports arrive 24-48 hrs after inspection ends

How it works

Three steps. Days instead of weeks.

From PO creation to final release, the flow stays in one system. Everyone sees the same facts. Nothing falls through the cracks.

01

Brand creates the PO

Define style, specs, quantities, inspection level, factory, and 3P inspector. System auto-generates the inspection schedule and sampling plan based on ISO 2859-1.

02

Factory produces · Inspector verifies

Factory logs in-line defects via mobile. 3P inspector arrives on scheduled date, pulls AQL samples, logs findings with photos. Everything syncs even without wifi.

03

Brand releases or raises NCR

Live Cost of Quality dashboard shows every defect, every cost impact. Brand approves release — or raises an NCR with workflow that routes to factory CAPA.

Common questions

Everything a sourcing or QA director wants to know.

We've probably been asked. Here are honest answers.

What languages does the platform support?

Eight at launch: English, Chinese (Simplified), Bangla, Vietnamese, Hindi, Turkish, Spanish, and Portuguese (Brazilian). More languages added quarterly based on which markets our customers operate in.

Factory-floor interfaces use pictorial defect codes so even workers who aren't literate in the system language can log issues accurately.

Will my factory workers actually use this on their phones?

Yes — because we built it for them, not for IT. The factory mobile app runs on $100 Android devices. Shared kiosks let workers log in with a 4-digit PIN. Pictorial defect codes mean no typing needed.

Offline mode holds up to 48 hours of data if wifi drops — common in older factory buildings. Everything syncs automatically when the connection returns.

What about data privacy — my factories don't want other brands seeing their numbers.

Multi-tenant isolation with row-level security. Each brand sees their own POs, their own factories' performance for their orders — never another brand's data. Factories choose what they share and with whom.

Infrastructure: AWS in four regions (US · EU · APAC · India). SOC 2 Type II target: Year 2. GDPR + India DPDP compliant from day one.

Can I integrate with my existing ERP or PLM?

Yes. Prebuilt connectors for SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics, Centric PLM, Lectra, and Infor M3. Open REST API + webhooks for anything else.

Typical integration: PO data flows in from your PLM, inspection results flow back to your ERP for accounting and vendor scorecards. Setup takes 2-4 weeks with our team.

How do you measure Cost of Quality?

Four buckets, calculated per PO, per factory, per season: Prevention (training, inspection infrastructure), Appraisal (inspections, lab tests), Internal failure (rework, scrap, downgrading), External failure (returns, chargebacks, markdowns, lost customer trust).

You see the live total, trend lines, and benchmarks across your supplier base. The platform's job: shift spending from failure buckets to prevention and appraisal.

Who pays — the brand, the factory, or the inspector?

Brand-sponsored model. The brand pays based on PO volume. Factories and 3rd-party inspectors use the platform free — because asking a factory to pay $500/month per brand they work with would make adoption impossible.

Pricing scales with usage. Pilot customers get introductory rates and co-design input.

When does it launch? What's available today?

Today: live clickable prototype, blueprint complete, pilot customer discovery underway. Private beta: Q2 2027 with 3-5 pilot brands. General availability: Q4 2027.

If you want to be a pilot customer, you'll get a discount on year 1, direct product input, and we'll build what you need against your actual workflows.

Is this a stealth-stage startup? Should I take the risk?

Yes — we're pre-launch. Founder-led, fully committed, 15+ years of apparel supply-chain operating experience behind the vision. Blueprint is locked, prototype is live, pitch deck is ready.

Pilot customers get: direct founder access, discounted year-1 pricing, co-design authority on roadmap, and the ability to shape a tool that serves them instead of being handed down from a 50-person product team two years from now.
