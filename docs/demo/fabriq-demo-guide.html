Interview the demo-taker

If you're showing this to someone else — ask these questions.

These are the Mom Test questions. They surface real pain points instead of polite agreement. Each one asks about past behavior, not hypothetical future actions.

Q1

"How do you handle quality issues today?"

Reveals the current workflow and tools. Listen for spreadsheets, WhatsApp, email, specific software names.

Q2

"Tell me about the last time quality went wrong."

Specific story. Specific pain. Numbers. "Last month we had to air-freight 10,000 units because…" is gold.

Q3

"What's the hardest part of your quality work?"

Lets them name their own biggest pain, in their own words. Builds the vocabulary we use in sales calls.

Q4

"If this worked exactly as shown — would it replace what you use today?"

Direct test. Watch for hesitation. "Only if…" tells you the real barrier.

Q5

"Who else should we talk to?"

Great referral prompt. Even negative demo responses lead to great introductions.

Q6

"What would you expect to pay for something like this?"

Specific numbers reveal perceived value. "A few hundred a month" vs "it's priceless" means very different things.
