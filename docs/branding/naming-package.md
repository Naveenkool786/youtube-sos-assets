## 1. Why Naming Matters More Than You Think

Your name will appear ~1,000,000 times over the company's life — in URLs, email signatures, invoices, legal documents, press articles, Slack notifications, 100 customer dashboards. The cost of changing it later is enormous (lost SEO, confused customers, re-branded apps, re-signed contracts).

Pick now, with a framework, under 2 weeks. Then commit.

## 2. Naming Strategy — What We're Optimizing For

### Must-haves
- **Pronounceable** across our 8 launch locales (EN, ZH, BN, VI, HI, TR, ES, PT-BR)
- **Short** (ideally 4–8 letters, max 10)
- **Spellable** on first hearing (no "is that Q-u-a-l-i-a or Q-u-a-l-y-a?")
- **.com available** OR acceptable alternative (.io, .ai, .co)
- **No major trademark conflicts** in US, EU, India (your initial markets)
- **Not confused with competitors** (ETQ, Qualio, Centric, Inspectorio, MasterControl)
- **Scales beyond apparel** (don't name yourself out of future verticals)
- **No embarrassing meanings** in major markets (critical cross-cultural check)

### Strong-plus
- **Metaphorically evocative** of quality / precision / clarity / verification
- **Easy visual logo potential** (short word with distinctive letterforms)
- **Google-able** (not too generic, not already SEO-crowded)
- **Memorable after one hearing** (test: say it in a sentence, does the listener re-say it correctly?)

### Nice-to-haves
- Industry nod (textile, thread, weave, loom) — gives domain credibility
- Latin root (travels well internationally)
- Works as a verb ("let's Zoom", "let's Slack it") — strong indicator of brand power

### Red flags to avoid
- ❌ Starting with Q (crowded space: Qualio, Quala, Quartz, Qlik, Qase, Quintly...)
- ❌ Pure descriptive names (QualityHQ, GarmentCheck) — hard to trademark, feel generic
- ❌ Numbers in name (looks like a startup fad: Oracle9, Base44)
- ❌ Hyphens or compound-case-weirdness
- ❌ Names that read as acronyms (ESLS, QMPL)
- ❌ Hard-to-type combos (double-z, uncommon diphthongs)
- ❌ Pop-culture references that date fast

## 4. Scoring Framework

For every finalist name, score 1–5 on each dimension. Sum = ranking.

| Dimension | Weight | 1 = bad | 5 = great |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Memorability** | 3× | Forgets after 1 min | Recalls after 1 day |
| **Pronounceability** (across 8 locales) | 3× | Mangled in Bengali/Vietnamese | Said correctly on first try |
| **Spellability** | 2× | Needs explanation ("Q-U-A-L-Y-A") | Spelled correctly on first hearing |
| **Domain availability (.com)** | 3× | .com for $100k+ | .com registrable < $100 |
| **Trademark clearance** (US + EU preliminary) | 3× | Direct conflict | Clear |
| **Brand fit** (quality / trust / modern) | 2× | Doesn't feel on-brand | Perfectly on-brand |
| **Scales beyond apparel** | 2× | Boxed into textiles | Works for any vertical |
| **Visual logo potential** | 1× | Awkward letters | Strong distinctive shape |
| **Cross-cultural safety** | 3× | Embarrassing in another language | Neutral/positive everywhere |

**Max score:** 110

## 6. Domain Availability — How to Check (This Week)

### Primary tools (free)
| Tool | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| [who.is](https://who.is) | Current owner of any domain |
| [instantdomainsearch.com](https://instantdomainsearch.com) | Live availability across TLDs |
| [domainr.com](https://domainr.com) | Shows alternatives + prices |
| [namecheap.com](https://www.namecheap.com) | Buy if available |
| [godaddy.com](https://www.godaddy.com) | Alt registrar |

### What to check for each shortlisted name

| TLD | Priority | If taken, workaround |
|---|---|---|
| **.com** | Must-have ideal | Try to buy from current owner (budget: $500–50k) |
| **.io** | Acceptable 2nd | Common in tech SaaS |
| **.ai** | Acceptable 3rd | Trendy, more expensive (~$50–200/yr) |
| **.co** | Acceptable 4th | Slight confusion risk with .com |
| **try[name].com**, **get[name].com**, **[name]hq.com**, **[name]app.com** | Last resort | Signals the main .com is taken |

### Backup trick
If `[name].com` is taken but domain is parked (not actively used), try to buy via escrow:
- Sedo.com (broker)
- DomainAgents (reach out via WHOIS email)
- Budget: $500–5,000 for parked domains, $10k–100k+ for premium

### Red flag
If `[name].com` goes to an active business — **move on**. Trademark fights get expensive fast.

## 7. Trademark Preliminary Check

### Free preliminary checks (do first)

| Jurisdiction | Tool | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **US** | [uspto.gov TESS search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov) | Primary market |
| **EU** | [TMview.europa.eu](https://www.tmview.europa.eu) | Covers all EU |
| **India** | [tmrsearch.ipindia.gov.in](https://tmrsearch.ipindia.gov.in) | Your home market |
| **UK** | [gov.uk/search-for-trademark](https://www.gov.uk/search-for-trademark) | Separate from EU post-Brexit |
| **China** | [tmsearch.cnipa.gov.cn](http://tmsearch.cnipa.gov.cn) | Important for apparel factories |

### How to search
1. Search exact name
2. Search phonetic variants (Verity → Veritee, Veraty)
3. Search in **Nice Classes** relevant to your business:
   - **Class 9** — software, SaaS, mobile apps
   - **Class 35** — business services, supply chain management
   - **Class 42** — software-as-a-service, SaaS
   - **Class 45** — compliance, inspection services

### Interpretation
| Result | Action |
|---|---|
| No marks found | ✅ Proceed to professional TM search |
| Marks found in unrelated classes (e.g., Class 25 clothing — different from Class 42 SaaS) | ⚠️ Likely OK, consult TM attorney |
| Marks found in same class or related classes | ❌ Pick another name |
| Famous mark exists in ANY class (e.g., "Nike") | ❌ Pick another name |

### After preliminary — hire a TM attorney
- Preliminary searches are **not legal clearance** — you need an attorney for actual filing
- Cost: $500–2,000 for professional TM search + opinion
- Cost: $500–2,500 per class per jurisdiction for filing
- Recommended: file in US first (fast, cheap), then EU + India + CN later

- "Nova" (star) in Spanish-speaking markets sounds like "no va" (doesn't go) — Chevy Nova sales were weak in Latin America
- "Fitta" is a cool-sounding name in English but offensive in Scandinavian languages
- "Mitsubishi Pajero" didn't launch under that name in Spanish-speaking markets

- **Day 13:** Commission a cheap logo on Fiverr for your top 2 ($50–200), see which lives

### Red flags to kill a name late in the process
- You cringe when saying it out loud 20 times in a row
- 3+ advisors ask "what does it mean?"
- Native speaker says "this sounds weird in [language]"
- You keep searching for alternatives

### Green flags to lock it in
- Feels natural within 48 hours of using it
- Advisors remember and re-use it without prompting
- You can imagine the logo clearly
- You've already started referring to the company as "[Name]"

## 11. Legal + Registration Next Steps

### Order of operations once name is picked

1. **Domain purchase** (same day; $10–100 for standard, up to $5k for premium)
2. **Trademark attorney engagement** (within 1 week; $500–2k)
3. **Trademark filing** US (within 2 weeks; $350 USPTO fee + $500–1k attorney)
4. **Legal entity name reservation** (file with your state/country; $50–500)
5. **Social handles grab** (same day; free)
6. **GitHub organization** (free)
7. **Google Workspace** with the new domain ($6–12/user/mo)
8. **Pitch deck update** — replace [COMPANY NAME] with real name
9. **Prototype update** — replace QMS placeholder
10. **CLAUDE.md update** — add brand name reference

### Entity + name interaction
- US LLC/C-Corp: file "Doing Business As" (DBA) if entity name differs
- India PLC: name is the entity name (chosen at incorporation)
- Common pattern: entity = "[Name], Inc." + product/brand = "[Name]"

## 14. Common Founder Naming Mistakes to Avoid

1. ❌ **Falling in love with a name before checking availability** — breaks your heart
2. ❌ **Picking a name only your co-founders like** — you need advisors + target customers to react positively
3. ❌ **Over-indexing on cleverness** — "QualityStitch" is clever but corny
4. ❌ **Choosing a name you can't afford to defend in trademark** — avoid conflicts with Fortune 500 brands
5. ❌ **Picking a name you can't pronounce confidently in a pitch** — if you hesitate on your own name, investors will notice
6. ❌ **Chasing the perfect name indefinitely** — "good enough + available" beats "perfect + unobtainable" every time
7. ❌ **Ignoring the sound test** — say the name 100 times before committing
8. ❌ **Buying a name without TM clearance** — you may have to rebrand after year 1
